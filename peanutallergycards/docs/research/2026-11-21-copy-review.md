# Copy and sections review vs Shopify stores

_2026-11-21. Headings, button labels, page titles and word counts extracted
from the live pages of Graza, Great Jones, Ghia, Magic Spoon and Allbirds,
compared with variant 2 (`/v2`, `/v2/thailand`). Patterns only; no copy taken._

## What they do

- **Short, benefit-led headlines,** often two beats: "Childhood Classics.
  Grown-Up Ingredients." (Magic Spoon), "Our biggest sale. Your comfiest
  season." (Allbirds), "Extra Virgin Olive Oil makes everything better." (Graza).
- **The offer is everywhere:** announcement bars ("Free Shipping On Orders
  $60+"), header buttons ("Bundle & save up to 26%"), tags on products.
- **Many specific buttons:** 14–29 per homepage ("Shop The Trio", "Build
  your own bundle", "Shop Now" on every product tile).
- **Short product nicknames as the page heading** ("The Duo", "The Dutchess",
  "The Discovery Set"); keywords live in the browser title instead.
- **Transparency details** that build trust: Graza's "Now serving olives
  from: Andalusia, Spain · Harvesting season October–January".
- **Product pages:** a "Why it's special" heading over 3 bullets, then tabs or
  accordions: Details, materials, how to use, shipping and returns.
- **Founder or maker story** ("Made by people who take olives very
  seriously."), a **guarantee** ("Happiness 100% Guaranteed"), and **content**
  (recipes, a blog) that keeps people coming back.
- **Voice:** warm, confident, a little playful ("We're booze free, but our
  emails are intoxicating").
- Homepages run about 370–1,450 words; product pages about 800–1,700.

## How we compared, and what changed

| Area             | Before                                                      | After                                                                                                     |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Browser titles   | "Shop peanut allergy cards (variant 2)"                     | Keyword-led, e.g. "Thai Peanut Allergy Card for Thailand: Chef Card + Emergency Card"                     |
| Announcement bar | "Instant download · printed sets include the digital files" | "Trip pack: 3 countries, save 25% · Instant download · Every card checked by a native speaker"            |
| Header           | Nav + cart                                                  | Plus a "Trip pack · save 25%" button                                                                      |
| Hero copy        | "…Show it, get a clear yes or no…"                          | "…Hand it over, get a clear yes or no from the kitchen, and enjoy the meal. No guessing, no polite nods." |
| Buttons          | 2 on the homepage                                           | Plus a "See the [country] cards" button on every tile                                                     |
| Trust            | none beyond claims                                          | **The translation desk:** each card's real review status, from the content data                           |
| Product heading  | "Thailand Peanut Allergy Card Set"                          | "The Thailand Set" + "Peanut allergy chef card + emergency card · Thai and English"                       |
| Product details  | 3 accordions                                                | "Why it works" heading; new Details and Delivery accordions                                               |
| Email sign-up    | "New countries and travel tips, straight to you."           | "Be first to know when your country is ready."                                                            |

Headlines that already matched the best of theirs were kept: "Eat abroad
with confidence.", "One card. One question. A clear yes.", "How a meal goes",
"Why not just use an app?", "Going to more than one country?".

## Still missing (needs the owner)

1. **Founder story:** who is behind this and why (2–3 sentences of fact).
   This is the strongest trust section the stores have that we don't.
2. **Guarantee:** for example, "If a translation is wrong, we'll fix it and
   refund you." A business decision.
3. **Card size and shipping** for the Details and Delivery sections
   (currently "to be confirmed").
4. **Reviews:** only once real customers exist.
5. **Guides** (the stores' recipes and blogs, for us): e.g. "Eating in
   Thailand with a peanut allergy". Good for search, but needs careful,
   checked content.
