# Shopify store patterns

_Researched 2026-11-21 by screenshotting the homepage and a product page of
five stores from Shopify's own 2026 design showcase: Graza, Magic Spoon, Ghia,
Great Jones and Allbirds. Used for design variant 2 (`/v2`)._

## Product page patterns

- Large gallery with a thumbnail rail (vertical on desktop).
- Title in the display face; star rating and review count right under it.
- Price with a struck-through compare-at price when discounted.
- Options as picture tiles, with small tags ("New!", "Most popular").
- Quantity breaks with discount tags (Graza: 1/2/3/6 sets; Magic Spoon: 4/6/8 packs, "Save up to 26%").
- One-time vs subscribe radio (not relevant to us).
- Full-width add-to-cart button, then a trust row (free shipping, 30-day trial).
- "Why it's special" bullets, then accordions or tabs for details.
- Below the fold: an explainer told with illustrations (Graza's "1 olive, 2 very different oils"), cross-sells, the full reviews list, and customer photos.
- Sticky add-to-cart bar once the main button scrolls away (Great Jones).

## What we adapted

- **Quantity breaks → multi-country savings** (1 / 2 / 3 countries, save 15% / 25%; placeholder numbers).
- **"1 olive, 2 oils" → "One card, two sides"**: the local-language front next to the English back.
- **Comparison table** against free printable cards and translation apps (kinds, not named brands).
- **No fake social proof.** There are no reviews yet, so the product page says "No reviews yet" and the reviews section shows clearly labelled placeholders.
