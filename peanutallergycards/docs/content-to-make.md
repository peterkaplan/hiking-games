# Content to make

Guides, stories and pages to write: the store's version of Graza's recipes
and blog, or Ghia's "sips on repeat". They bring in search traffic, give people
a reason to come back, and show we know the subject.

Search phrases come from the
[search demand research](research/2026-11-20-search-demand.md).

## Rules for every piece

- **Safety first.** Any food fact ("peanuts are common in X") or phrase in
  another language must be checked before publishing: food facts against
  reliable sources, translations by a native speaker (as for the cards).
- **No medical advice.** Point to an allergist, FARE or the local equivalent
  for anything medical (peanut oil tolerance, epinephrine, reactions).
- Link each guide to the matching destination's cards.
- Record sources at the bottom of each piece.
