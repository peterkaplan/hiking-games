# Shot list

Images to generate for the store. Each brief is written to be pasted into an
image generator as it stands, after the style block.

## The idea behind every image

**The peanut is what we keep out. The traveller is the hero.** In every image
the peanut is caught, kept out, or left behind. It is never comfortable, never
on holiday, never the star having a good time. Where there is no peanut, the
image shows the card doing its job in a restaurant.

## Rules

- **No text.** No lettering, labels, logos, signs, menus or watermarks.
  Generated lettering comes out garbled; reject any frame that has it.
- **No faces.** Hands, forearms and chest-down only. A generated person must
  never stand in for a customer or for Julia.
- **Blank cards only.** Where a card appears it is plain cream with nothing
  printed on it. The real card is added afterwards, from
  `src/content/cards`.
- **One peanut, not a pile**, unless the brief says otherwise. In its shell
  unless the brief says "shelled".
- Save each file under the name given, as PNG or JPEG at 1600px or more on
  the long side.

## Style block (paste before every brief)

> Photorealistic photograph, not an illustration or 3D render. Warm, hard
> directional sunlight from the upper left; one crisp, soft-edged shadow
> falling to the lower right. Shallow depth of field with the subject tack
> sharp. Matte surfaces; no gloss, glare or lens flare. Colours limited to
> deep forest green (#0e5540), mustard yellow (#ffc933), warm cream (#faeddf)
> and natural food, wood, stone and steel tones. No blue, pink, purple or
> neon. Clean, uncluttered composition with generous empty space around the
> subject. No text, lettering, logos, labels, signage or watermarks anywhere.
> No faces.
